What the family remembers

“It was near Alsace”

J. always said so. The FamilySearch tree's earliest Gochenauer, born near Zürich in 1605, died in Alsace in 1685. Swiss Mennonites expelled from Zürich often settled there before sailing to Pennsylvania.

Possible
“Gochenouer is German”

J.'s answer fits: the name is Swiss-German, and the family lived in Germany for about a century, so they arrived in Pennsylvania as Germans. “Irish” doesn't fit the surname line, though Jane Wilson could carry a Scots-Irish thread.

The bald-head village

S. was born in her father's village, 光頭. Donald gave the family house to his cousin, and it was later sold to developers. Villages were one clan, so Donald's mother, a Chin, came from somewhere else.

From China to Jamaica
