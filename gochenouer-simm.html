What the family remembers

“It was near Alsace”

J. always said so. The FamilySearch tree's earliest Gochenauer, born near Zürich in 1605, died in Alsace in 1685. Swiss Mennonites expelled from Zürich often settled there before sailing to Pennsylvania.

Possible
“Gochenouer is German”

J.'s answer fits: the name is Swiss-German, and the family lived in Germany for about a century, so they arrived in Pennsylvania as Germans. “Irish” doesn't fit the surname line, though Jane Wilson could carry a Scots-Irish thread.

Probable
The village, and what drifted

S. remembered a sound, Gwong-tau, and a meaning — “bald head”. The sound was right and the characters had drifted: James Simm's own 1957 entry gives 崗頭 Gangtou at 觀瀾 Guanlan in 寶安 Bao'an, which is Gong-tiu in Hakka — ridge head. Donald gave the family house to a cousin; developers took it later.

Proven
The family was right

Iris was born 何 Ho in China and her name was 何運昭. The family said Ho for years against a US record that said Lowe — and a Kingston book of 1963, which nobody here had seen, named her 媳何運昭. Her clan came from 橫崗 Henggang, probably 茂盛 Maosheng.
